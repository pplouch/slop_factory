# Feature backlog 1: initial 17-item list

_Historical record for [Rogue idle slop factory survivor rpg ultimate](../../CLAUDE.md) — what landed and why, kept for provenance, not needed for day-to-day work._

## Feature backlog (multi-session)

The player handed over a 17-item feature/bug list in one go; it was worked through across multiple sessions, one commit per item (or tightly-related pair of items), each headless-verified (and, for anything mouse/UI-driven, also confirmed with a real-renderer screenshot) before committing. Sequencing choice: **foundations first** (refactors other items depend on), then whatever's quickest/most self-contained next, larger new-content/UI-design items last. **All 17 items are now done** (see below) — the list stays here as the record of what landed and why, without needing to re-derive it from `git log`. If the player hands over a new list, start a fresh section below this one rather than overwriting this history.

- [x] Map height isn't visible; should feel organic (valleys/mountains/fields) — root cause was amplitude, not the noise shape: `Biomes.height_noise` already had multi-octave fractal detail, but per-biome `height_amplitude` (0.12-0.5) was too subtle to read from the steep RTS camera. Added a second, biome-independent `macro_height_noise` layer (low frequency, larger amplitude) summed on top for multi-chunk valleys/mountains, and increased (but narrowed the *range* of) per-biome amplitudes -- narrowing the range specifically to keep the real geometric seam at biome-differing chunk borders small (a chunk is assigned one fixed biome, so the shared height_noise value gets multiplied by two different amplitudes on either side of such a border; verified empirically that the macro layer, not this seam, is what drives the large ridges/valleys now visible, by comparing screenshots before/after narrowing the per-biome range at the same map location and seeing the landform unchanged).

Verified no biome's `resources` list references a water scene and no "puddle" scattered-spawn code remains — just a stale checkbox, no code change needed.

Verified visually (real-renderer screenshots, not just headless) that the 3D mask and the minimap always agree, and that the 3D plane's edge coincides with the existing bounded (not map-wide) fog coverage rather than introducing a new limitation.
